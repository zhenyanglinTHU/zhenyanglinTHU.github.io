研究者简介 Profile

连接社会科学问题与人工智能系统 Connecting social questions with AI systems

以复杂系统为方法底座，以社会机制为研究对象，以数据和工程系统为验证路径。 Complex systems provide the methodology, social mechanisms define the questions, and data-intensive engineering provides the path to validation.

林镇阳为清华大学计算机科学与技术系博士后、工程物理系工学博士，兼修清华大学马克思主义学院政治经济学法学硕士，具有电子工程、复杂系统、人工智能与制度研究的交叉背景。 曾历任央企及科技上市公司研究院院长、数据银行事业部总经理、首席科学家等职务，形成了从前沿研究、技术平台建设到组织管理与产业化落地的完整实践经验。

研究聚焦社会世界模型、多智能体社会模拟、社会 AI Data 与计算治理，探索人工智能如何形成对社会状态、主体认知、互动机制与制度约束的可计算表征，并通过现实数据持续校准，支持机制解释、不确定性分析与反事实实验。

Zhenyang Lin completed postdoctoral research in Computer Science and Technology and received his Ph.D. in Engineering Physics from Tsinghua University, alongside formal graduate training in political economy. He previously served in roles including research institute dean, general manager of a data banking business unit, and chief scientist across central state-owned and publicly listed technology enterprises.

His research focuses on social world models, multi-agent social simulation, social AI data, and computational governance. The central goal is to build computable representations of social states, cognition, interaction, and institutions that can be calibrated with real-world data.

研究方向 Research themes

从主体认知到社会涌现 From cognition to emergence

围绕“可解释、可校准、可反事实、可验证”四个目标，建设社会智能的模型、数据与实验基础设施。 We build models, data, and experimental infrastructure for explainable, calibrated, counterfactual, and verifiable social intelligence.

01

社会世界模型 Social world models

社会状态表征、社会动力学、跨层级涌现、机制推理、反事实模拟与不确定性量化。 State representation, social dynamics, multi-scale emergence, mechanistic reasoning, counterfactual simulation, and uncertainty.

02

多智能体社会模拟 Multi-agent social simulation

认知智能体、记忆与关系网络、制度约束、大规模交互及群体行为涌现。 Cognitive agents, memory and relational networks, institutional constraints, large-scale interaction, and collective behavior.

03

社会 AI Data Social AI data

社会轨迹、互动链、机制标注、合成数据以及模型校准与评测基准。 Social trajectories, interaction chains, mechanism annotations, synthetic data, calibration, and benchmarks.

04

计算治理与政策实验 Computational governance

面向公共治理、舆情传播、产业协同和数据基础制度开展计算实验。 Computational experiments for public governance, information diffusion, industrial coordination, and data institutions.

技术路线 Research program

从模拟器走向社会世界模型 From simulators to social world models

模拟生成可研究的社会过程，数据连接真实反馈，世界模型学习机制并支持反事实推演，真实场景再反向校准研究。 Simulation creates observable social processes, data brings real-world feedback, world models learn mechanisms and enable counterfactuals, and deployment closes the calibration loop.

01

社会模拟器 Social simulator

构建主体、关系、环境与制度规则。 Represent agents, relations, environments, and rules.

02

社会 AI Data Social AI data

沉淀轨迹、交互、反馈与机制数据。 Capture trajectories, interactions, feedback, and mechanisms.

03

社会世界模型 Social world model

学习社会动力学并进行因果与反事实推演。 Learn social dynamics for causal and counterfactual reasoning.

04

真实场景验证 Real-world validation

通过治理与产业问题持续校准模型。 Calibrate models through governance and industrial settings.
