01

数据要素价值化与融通平台 Data value creation and exchange infrastructure

针对数据流通利用面临的确权评估难、可信融通难、存储能耗高等“两难一高”挑战，带领团队研发全国首个面向数据要素价值化的数据融通平台，创新提出数据“收、存、治、用、易”全生命周期价值化机制。相关成果获2023年度国家科学技术进步奖二等奖、2023年度北京市科学技术进步奖二等奖（数据要素类唯一项目），并于2022年、2023年两次入选工业和信息化部大数据产业发展示范名单。

Led the development of China's first data exchange platform designed for data-element value creation, addressing difficult ownership and valuation, trusted exchange, and high storage energy consumption. The work introduced a lifecycle mechanism spanning data collection, storage, governance, use, and exchange. Related outcomes received Second Prizes of the 2023 State Science and Technology Progress Award and the 2023 Beijing Science and Technology Progress Award—the only project in the data-elements category, and were selected for the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology's Big Data Industry Development Demonstration List in both 2022 and 2023.

02

多模态数智融合认知计算 Multimodal data-intelligence cognitive computing

聚焦多模态数智融合认知计算技术研发，主导研发“易境”可信数据空间一体机、“天枢”数据流通平台、“策渊”高质量数据集建设运营综合服务平台，融合数智技术与区块链技术，为多源异构、多模态数据的安全流通与合规应用提供支撑。作为专家组组长，牵头发布国内首部《高质量数据集建设及运营前沿报告（2025）》，构建多层次高质量数据集、知识库及“采、炼、测、用”一体化知识管理体系；协同华为、火山引擎等产业伙伴，推动 AI+智慧园区、城市视频分析等项目在湖北、福建等地落地。

Directed R&D in multimodal data-intelligence cognitive computing, including the Yijing trusted data space appliance, Tianshu data circulation platform, and Ceyuan high-quality dataset development and operations platform. These systems combine intelligent data technologies with blockchain to support secure circulation and compliant use of heterogeneous multimodal data. As expert group chair, led the publication of China's first Frontier Report on High-Quality Dataset Development and Operations (2025), established an integrated collect-refine-evaluate-use knowledge management framework, and worked with partners including Huawei and Volcano Engine to deploy AI solutions for smart parks and urban video analytics in Hubei, Fujian, and other regions.

03

“数据银行”理论与政务数据运营 Data Bank theory and public-data operations

提出的“数据银行”数据要素价值化理论于2021年在江西省抚州市落地，形成全国首个基于“数据银行”的政务数据授权运营模式，先后获《光明日报》、新华网等中央媒体报道。该模式随后在河南、内蒙古、雄安、江西、郑州、成都、开封、无锡、淮安等地推广应用，产生直接经济价值数亿元。

The Data Bank theory of data-element value creation was implemented in Fuzhou, Jiangxi, in 2021, creating China's first authorized public-data operating model based on a Data Bank. The model was reported by Guangming Daily, Xinhua News, and other national media, and was subsequently replicated in Henan, Inner Mongolia, Xiong'an, Jiangxi, Zhengzhou, Chengdu, Kaifeng, Wuxi, Huai'an, and other locations, generating several hundred million yuan in direct economic value.

04

数据资产入表服务体系 Data-asset balance-sheet recognition services

牵头组建全国首个数据资产入表服务联合体，从法律、安全、价值与财务等专业维度，为客户提供数据合规、安全评估、质量评价、价值评估、会计审查、数据交易到数据资本化的全流程服务；牵头编写并发布全国首部《数据资源入表白皮书》，服务全国首批数据资产评估试点企业。

Established China's first service consortium for recognizing data assets on balance sheets, integrating legal, security, valuation, and financial expertise across compliance, security assessment, quality evaluation, valuation, accounting review, data transactions, and capitalization. Also led the publication of China's first White Paper on Recognizing Data Resources on Balance Sheets and supported the country's first cohort of pilot enterprises for data-asset valuation.
