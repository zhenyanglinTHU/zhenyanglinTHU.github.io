加入我们 Join

我们正在建设“社会世界模型与计算社会系统”研究方向，欢迎对复杂社会问题有好奇心、愿意把想法变成可验证研究的同学。 We are building a research program on social world models and computational social systems, and welcome students who want to turn curiosity about society into testable research.

这里既有基础问题，也有真实系统：你可以研究社会表征、智能体认知与交互、群体涌现和反事实推理，也可以建设数据集、评测基准、仿真基础设施和面向现实问题的实验平台。我们重视严谨性、工程能力和对现实机制的尊重，希望每位成员都能形成可验证、可复现、可交流的成果。

The group works on both foundational questions and real systems: social representation, agent cognition and interaction, collective emergence, counterfactual reasoning, datasets, benchmarks, simulation infrastructure, and experimental platforms. We value rigor, engineering ability, and respect for real social mechanisms.

研究切口 Research entry points

从一个具体问题开始 Start from a concrete question

不要求所有同学拥有相同背景。重要的是能把自己的方法优势带到共同问题中，并愿意理解其他学科的证据标准。 Students need not share the same background. What matters is bringing a methodological strength to shared questions and learning the evidence standards of other fields.

A / MODELS

社会世界模型 Social world models

社会状态表征、动力学学习、因果与反事实推理、不确定性、模型校准。 Social state representation, dynamics, causal and counterfactual reasoning, uncertainty, calibration.

B / AGENTS

多智能体社会模拟 Multi-agent social simulation

大语言模型智能体、认知架构、复杂网络、大规模仿真与并行系统。 LLM agents, cognitive architectures, complex networks, large-scale simulation, parallel systems.

C / DATA

社会 AI Data Social AI data

数据生成、轨迹对齐、反馈学习、数据质量、评测与数据治理。 Data generation, trajectory alignment, feedback learning, quality, evaluation, and governance.

D / GOVERNANCE

计算治理 Computational governance

公共政策、舆情传播、产业与组织、数据制度和跨学科计算实验。 Public policy, information diffusion, organizations, data institutions, interdisciplinary experiments.

适合背景 Backgrounds

技术能力与社会问题意识并重 Technical depth and social problem awareness

欢迎来自计算机、人工智能、复杂系统、数学、物理、经济学、社会学、公共管理、法学及相关专业的同学。 We welcome backgrounds in computer science, AI, complex systems, mathematics, physics, economics, sociology, public administration, law, and related fields.

研究习惯 Research habits

愿意提出明确问题、阅读文献并检验假设。 Form clear questions, read deeply, and test hypotheses.

工程能力 Engineering

能够把算法、数据或系统做成可运行实验。 Turn algorithms, data, or systems into working experiments.

跨学科沟通 Interdisciplinary work

尊重不同学科的概念、证据与伦理边界。 Respect concepts, evidence, and ethical boundaries.

长期主义 Long-term thinking

重视过程积累、复现质量和持续学习。 Value cumulative work, reproducibility, and learning.

申请方式 How to apply

让我们快速理解你的能力和兴趣 Help us understand your strengths and interests

招生类型与正式流程以北京中关村学院当期通知为准。前期交流可通过邮件发起。 Official admission categories and procedures follow the current notices of Beijing Zhongguancun Academy. Initial conversations may begin by email.

  1. 准备简历 Curriculum vitae

    包括教育经历、研究或项目经历、技能与成果。 Education, research or projects, skills, and outputs.

  2. 课程与成绩信息 Course and academic record

    提交成绩单，或提供与申请方向相关的课程清单。 A transcript or a course list relevant to the proposed direction.

  3. 1–2项代表作 One or two best works

    论文、代码、项目报告、竞赛成果或能够体现能力的作品。 Papers, code, project reports, competitions, or other evidence of ability.

  4. 一页研究兴趣说明 One-page research statement

    说明想研究的问题、已有准备，以及希望从课题组获得什么训练。 The question you want to study, your preparation, and the training you seek.

常见问题 FAQ

关于方向、背景与产业转化 Directions, backgrounds, and translation

如果你的问题涉及当年招生政策，请以学院正式通知为准。 For questions about admissions policy in a specific year, please follow official academy notices.

没有社会科学背景可以申请吗？ Can I apply without a social science background?

可以。计算机、人工智能、数学、物理和复杂系统背景都很适合，但需要愿意学习社会科学概念、研究设计和伦理规范。 Yes. Computer science, AI, mathematics, physics, and complex systems are all relevant, provided you are willing to learn social science concepts, research design, and ethics.

社会科学专业但编程经验不多，可以申请吗？ Can social science students with limited programming experience apply?

可以。扎实的问题意识、理论基础、实证方法和数据经验同样重要。建议在申请材料中说明目前的技术基础和学习计划。 Yes. Strong questions, theory, empirical methods, and data experience are valuable. Explain your current technical foundation and learning plan in the application.

学生是否必须参与鉴远智能的项目？ Must students participate in Jianyuan projects?

不必须。学生培养和学术研究是课题组的核心。任何涉及企业项目的参与都应基于自愿、适配研究目标，并遵守学院规定及清晰的知识产权、数据和发表边界。 No. Education and academic research are central. Any company project participation should be voluntary, aligned with research goals, and governed by academy rules and explicit IP, data, and publication boundaries.

可以申请实习、科研助理或访问交流吗？ Are internships, research assistant roles, or visits available?

可通过邮件说明希望参与的类型、时间和研究方向。是否开放取决于当期课题、学院安排和指导容量。 Email the requested format, timing, and research direction. Availability depends on current projects, academy arrangements, and supervision capacity.

期待看到你真正想解决的问题 Tell us about the problem you genuinely want to solve

一封清楚、具体、诚实的邮件，比堆叠术语更能开启一次有价值的交流。 A clear, specific, honest message starts a better conversation than a stack of buzzwords.
